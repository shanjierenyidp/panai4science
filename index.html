Our Mission

Our mission is to develop AI-enabled computational technologies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computational physics, and geometry modeling. We create scalable, physics-aware methods for understanding, predicting, and designing complex physical and biomedical systems, with an emphasis on scientific discovery, reliable digital twins, and real-world impact.
